02 Patents

Two US patents in automated LiDAR target simulation.

Test a LiDAR sensor without physical targets.

The invention automatically scans a simulated target across a LiDAR sensor's field of view, so the sensor can be tested end to end in the lab instead of against real objects at real distances.

Developed while I was the sole software developer on Keysight's LiDAR Target Simulator project, later commercialized as the E8717A.
